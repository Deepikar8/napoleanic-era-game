# The Surrender at Ulm — 17 October 1805

There will be no battle today. Mack is trapped inside Ulm with thirty thousand men. Four roads still run out of the city — north, east, south, and west — and so long as one is open, an army can slip away.

Close all four. The Austrian army dissolves on the next sunrise.

*This is a maneuver puzzle: occupy ALL FOUR road tiles around Ulm — (4, 3), (6, 4), (4, 6), and (3, 4) — within six turns. Mack will not attack; he is already negotiating his surrender. There are no casualties to take. There is only geometry, and you have five corps for four exits.*
